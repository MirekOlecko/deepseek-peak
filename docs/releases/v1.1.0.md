Peak and off-peak now follow DeepSeek's published rule exactly, including the
"excluding Chinese public holidays" clause that the previous release did not model.

### Download

Choose the **DMG** and drag DeepSeekPeak.app into Applications, or download the ZIP.
Requires **macOS 13+**, with both **Apple Silicon and Intel** supported by one universal
app. Version **1.1.0**.

### What changed

- **Chinese public holidays are off-peak in full.** A weekday holiday no longer shows a
  peak window or a "peak starts in" countdown. Dates for 2025 and 2026 come from the
  State Council notices and are bundled.
- **The holiday is named in the widget**, for example
  "National Day in China (Oct 1-7) - peak suspended", and a skipped peak posts a
  notification instead of staying silent.
- **`holidays.json`** adds or corrects a year without rebuilding the app (menu →
  **Edit holiday dates**). A year in that file replaces the bundled year.
- **Unpublished years are reported, never guessed**: "2027 holiday dates missing -
  weekdays shown as peak" appears until the next notice is added.
- **`--status`** prints the loaded schedule, holiday years and the resulting state, and
  `--render ... --at <ISO8601>` renders the widget for any date.
- Peak rules are unchanged: 01:00–04:00 and 06:00–10:00 UTC, Monday to Friday, with
  off-peak at half the peak rate.

### First launch

This community release is **ad-hoc signed and not notarized by Apple**. If macOS blocks
it, follow **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway** after trying to launch
it. See [Apple's instructions](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102445). Managed Macs may
prevent this exception.

SHA256SUMS.txt contains checksums for both downloads. The DMG includes installation
instructions and the MIT license.

### Verification and accuracy

All 26 schedule and holiday tests passed, including a peak window split by a holiday that
starts at Beijing midnight. Both architectures and the app signature were verified, and
previews were rendered for a normal peak day, a National Day weekday and a day beyond the
last known holiday year.

The app uses a local schedule, not live billing information. Check
[DeepSeek's official pricing](https://api-docs.deepseek.com/quick_start/pricing/) for
current terms. This project is independent and not affiliated with DeepSeek.
